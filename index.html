Questions

About the demo, bookings and connecting your own backend.

Email a question

Is the demo connected to a real server?

No. It runs on sample data with a simulated server in your browser. Your changes stay in this browser until you press Reset demo.

How do I sign in as a member or organizer?

Switch roles in the top bar. Every demo account uses the password eventbook-demo-2027.

What happens when an event is full?

Members join the waitlist. A freed seat goes to the first in line for 24 hours, then to the next.

How long is a seat held at checkout?

15 minutes. If payment is not finished by then, the seat goes back on sale.

Can I connect my own backend?

Yes. Swap the methods in src/lib/server.js for fetch calls that return the same shape. A Supabase database schema ships with the code.
